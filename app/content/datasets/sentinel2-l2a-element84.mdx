    **Sentinel-2 Level-2A** is the European Space Agency's (ESA) analysis-ready, bottom-of-atmosphere (BOA) surface reflectance product derived from the **Multispectral Instrument (MSI)** flying on the Copernicus Sentinel-2 satellites. The Sentinel-2 constellation - Sentinel-2A (launched June 2015), Sentinel-2B (launched March 2017), and Sentinel-2C (launched September 2024) - delivers wide-swath, high-resolution, multispectral imagery in 13 spectral bands spanning the visible, near-infrared, and shortwave infrared. Level-2A scenes are atmospherically corrected from Level-1C top-of-atmosphere reflectance using the Sen2Cor processor and include a Scene Classification Layer (SCL) plus aerosol optical thickness (AOT) and water vapor (WVP) auxiliary products.

    This catalog entry surfaces the Sentinel-2 Level-2A archive as it is hosted by **[Element84](https://element84.com/)** on the **[Earth Search](https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1)** SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog (STAC) API. Earth Search indexes the [Sentinel-2 AWS Open Data bucket](https://registry.opendata.aws/sentinel-2-l2a-cogs/) as Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs (COGs), so each scene's individual band assets are directly tileable without pre-staging or download. The collection identifier on Earth Search is `sentinel-2-l2a`.

    ## Dataset Details
    - **Temporal Extent:** 27 June 2015 - present
    - **Temporal Resolution:** 5-day revisit at the equator with Sentinel-2A and 2B combined; 2-3 day effective revisit at mid- and high latitudes due to swath overlap
    - **Spatial Extent:** Global land surfaces, large islands, inland and coastal waters between approximately 84°N and 56°S
    - **Spatial Resolution:** 10 m (B02 blue, B03 green, B04 red, B08 NIR); 20 m (B05/B06/B07 red edge, B8A narrow NIR, B11/B12 SWIR); 60 m (B01 coastal aerosol, B09 water vapor)
    - **Tiling:** 100 km × 100 km tiles on the Military Grid Reference System (MGRS), in UTM / WGS84
    - **Atmospheric Correction:** Sen2Cor (Level-1C → Level-2A)
    - **Format:** Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF (COG), one asset per band

    ## Bands and Assets
    Each STAC item exposes the individual band assets `coastal`, `blue`, `green`, `red`, `rededge1`, `rededge2`, `rededge3`, `nir`, `nir08`, `nir09`, `swir16`, `swir22`, plus the auxiliary layers `aot` (aerosol optical thickness), `wvp` (water vapor), and `scl` (Scene Classification Layer with cloud and shadow masks). A pre-rendered 8-bit `visual` true-color asset and a `thumbnail` are also available. Surface reflectance values are stored as 16-bit integers scaled by 10000; divide by 10000 to recover physical reflectance in the 0-1 range. The visualization above renders the 10 m `red` (B04) asset.

    ## Common Applications
    Sentinel-2 L2A is the workhorse for high-cadence land monitoring at high resolution: vegetation indices and crop type / condition mapping (NDVI, EVI, NDRE leveraging the red-edge bands), forest disturbance and deforestation alerts, burned-area mapping, inland and coastal water quality, urban growth, and snow / ice extent. Its combination of 10 m resolution and a sub-weekly revisit makes it complementary to Landsat for change detection where both spatial detail and timeliness matter.
