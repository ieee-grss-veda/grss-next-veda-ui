    The **Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 Science Products** are the U.S. Geological Survey's (USGS) higher-level, analysis-ready output from more than four decades of Landsat observations. Level-2 products are derived from the Collection 2 Level-1 archive and include atmospherically corrected **Surface Reflectance (SR)** and **Surface Temperature (ST)** for scenes acquired by Landsat 4-5 Thematic Mapper (TM), Landsat 7 Enhanced Thematic Mapper Plus (ETM+), and Landsat 8-9 Operational Land Imager / Thermal Infrared Sensor (OLI/TIRS). Together these products provide the longest continuous, calibrated record of the Earth's land surface available from a civilian satellite program.

    This catalog entry surfaces the Collection 2 Level-2 archive as it is hosted by **[Element84](https://element84.com/)** on the **[Earth Search](https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1)** SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog (STAC) API. Element84's Earth Search indexes the [Landsat Collection 2 AWS Open Data bucket](https://registry.opendata.aws/usgs-landsat/) as Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs (COGs), making each scene's individual band assets directly tileable without any pre-staging or download. The collection identifier on Earth Search is `landsat-c2-l2`.

    ## Dataset Details
    - **Temporal Extent:** August 1982 - present (Landsat 4 launch through the active Landsat 8/9 mission)
    - **Temporal Resolution:** 16-day revisit per satellite; ~8-day effective revisit with Landsat 8 and Landsat 9 phased on opposite sides of the same orbit
    - **Spatial Extent:** Global land surface (185 km cross-track swath)
    - **Spatial Resolution:** 30 m for visible, near-infrared (NIR) and short-wave infrared (SWIR) bands; 15 m panchromatic (Landsat 7-9); thermal acquired at 100 m and resampled to 30 m
    - **Atmospheric Correction:** LaSRC (Landsat 8/9), LEDAPS (Landsat 4-7)
    - **Format:** Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF (COG), one asset per band
    - **Coordinate Reference System:** UTM, WGS84 (per scene)

    ## Bands and Assets
    Each STAC item exposes individual band assets including `coastal` (~0.44 µm), `blue` (~0.48 µm), `green` (~0.56 µm), `red` (~0.65 µm), `nir08` (~0.86 µm), `swir16` (~1.61 µm), and `swir22` (~2.20 µm), plus quality layers `qa_pixel`, `qa_radsat`, and `qa_aerosol`. Surface reflectance values are stored as scaled 16-bit integers; multiply by 0.0000275 and add -0.2 to recover physical reflectance in the 0-1 range.

    This dataset entry exposes four single-band visualizations covering the bands most often used in analytical workflows:

    - **Red Surface Reflectance** — visible red (~0.65 µm); part of true-color composites and an input to NDVI / EVI.
    - **Near-Infrared (NIR) Surface Reflectance** — `nir08` (~0.86 µm); the high-reflectance band for healthy vegetation and a primary input for vegetation indices and water-body masks.
    - **Shortwave Infrared 1 (SWIR1)** — `swir16` (~1.61 µm); sensitive to vegetation and soil moisture, and a component of the Normalized Burn Ratio (NBR).
    - **Shortwave Infrared 2 (SWIR2)** — `swir22` (~2.20 µm); used for burn-severity mapping (NBR), geological / mineral mapping, and discriminating snow from clouds.

    ## Common Applications
    Surface Reflectance and Surface Temperature underpin a wide range of land-monitoring workflows: vegetation indices and crop condition (NDVI, EVI, NDWI), land cover and land use change detection, burned-area mapping, surface water dynamics, urban heat island analysis, and snow and glacier monitoring. The four-decade archive makes Landsat C2 L2 particularly well suited to long-term trend analysis where consistency of calibration matters more than revisit frequency.
